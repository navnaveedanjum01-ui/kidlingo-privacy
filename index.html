Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Developer: Lvenia Apps

At a glance

  • No personal information collected
  • No accounts, sign-up or login
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Progress stays on the device
  • Payments handled only by Google Play
  • Parents' area protected by a parental gate

1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the KidLingo English app (Android package com.kidlingo.englishappforkids) handles information. The app is made by Lvenia Apps ("we", "us") and is designed for children aged 3 to 10 and their parents. We built KidLingo English so that children can learn and play without sharing any personal information with us.

2Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, sign-up or login. It never asks for, and we never receive, a child's or parent's name, email address, phone number, photo, voice recording, contacts, precise or approximate location, or advertising ID.

The app does not use the camera or the microphone and does not access photos, files, contacts or location.

3Information saved on the device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information only on the device it is installed on. This information is never sent to us.

What is savedWhy
Learning progress: stars earned and activities playedShows progress on the home screen and in the parents' progress report
Settings: sound effects and voice on or offRemembers the parent's choices
Whether the welcome screen was seenShows the welcome screen only once
How long the phone's voice takes to startMakes the start-up loading screen more accurate
When a "Rate us" message was last shownAvoids asking too often
A copy of the subscription confirmation from Google PlayLets the app keep working for up to 3 days without internet

This information is not included in device backups. Parents can erase learning progress at any time with Parents › Reset progress. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

4The voice

Words are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine that is built into the Android device. The app sends only the word or sentence to be spoken to that engine on the device. KidLingo English does not record or store any audio.

5Subscriptions and payments

KidLingo English offers the KidLingo Premium subscription, which starts with a free trial and then renews monthly. All purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store card numbers, bank details or billing addresses.

When a subscription is bought or restored, Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation. The app checks this confirmation on the device to unlock the activities. To provide its billing service, Google Play may also process technical information about the purchase. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time in the Google Play Store under Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

6Internet use and permissions

All learning content is built into the app and works offline. The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play about the subscription. It requests these permissions:

  • Google Play billing, to offer and check the subscription
  • Internet and network state, so Google Play billing can connect

7No advertising, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no social media features and no third-party tracking. The only third-party service the app uses is Google Play billing, described above. We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not collect any.

8Children's privacy

KidLingo English is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, as required by laws such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe.

Features meant for adults are inside the parents' area, which is protected by a parental gate (a short math question). These include settings, the progress report, subscriptions, sharing the app and rating it in the Play Store. Children cannot leave the app or make a purchase without passing the parental gate.

If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please email us at techfriend1124@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

9Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold any personal information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You stay in control of the information saved on your device: reset progress in the parents' area, or uninstall the app to remove it completely.

10Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Important changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
